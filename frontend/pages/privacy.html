Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This is a starting template, not legal advice. Have it reviewed against Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and registered with the Data Protection Commission before going live — most businesses that process personal data in Ghana are legally required to register as data controllers.

[Your Registered Company Name] ("SpotGH", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect through the Platform, why, and what rights you have over it.

1. Information We Collect

CategoryExamples
Account dataName, email, phone number, password (hashed), profile photo
Business dataBusiness name, address, hours, photos, products, pricing
Payment dataTransaction reference, amount, plan — full card details are handled by Paystack, not stored by us
Usage dataPages viewed, searches, clicks on listings, device/browser info, IP address
Location dataApproximate location if you enable "Near Me" search (with your permission)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and business listings;
  • To process subscription payments via Paystack;
  • To send transactional emails (receipts, booking confirmations, plan-expiry reminders) and, where you've opted in, marketing updates;
  • To show relevant search results and analytics to business owners about their own listing;
  • To detect fraud, abuse, and fake reviews, and to keep the Platform secure;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing Your Information

  • Paystack — to process payments;
  • Cloudinary — to host images you upload;
  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider;
  • Other users — your business listing content (name, photos, contact info, reviews you write) is public by design;
  • We don't sell your personal data to third parties for advertising.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences (like light/dark theme). We don't currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account and business data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we'll remove or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to keep records (e.g. payment records) for legal or accounting purposes.

6. Your Rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • Ask us to delete your data, subject to legal retention requirements;
  • Withdraw consent for optional data uses (like marketing emails) at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@spotgh.com.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures — encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access-controlled databases — to protect your data. No system is 100% secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

8. Children's Privacy

SpotGH isn't intended for children under 18. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via the Platform or by email.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email privacy@spotgh.com.
