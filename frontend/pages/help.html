How can we help?

🏪 Getting Listed

How to create a listing
  1. From your dashboard, choose Add Business, then pick the category that best matches your business — this also decides your mini-website's layout.
  2. Walk through the short wizard: Business Info (name, tagline), Location & Contact, Description, and Website (tell us if you already have one, or we'll build you a free mini-website).
  3. Review your details on the final step and hit Submit for Review.
  4. Your listing goes to pending status while our team reviews it — most are approved within a day.
  5. Once approved, head back in to add a logo, photos, and business hours — these aren't required to submit, but they make a big difference to how your listing performs.

Note: you'll need an active plan before you can create your first listing — see "How to upgrade a subscription" below.

How to manage a business website

If you asked SpotGH to build your mini-website, it's generated automatically from your listing — your logo, photos, description, products, and business hours all appear on it. To update it:

  1. Go to Dashboard → your business → Edit.
  2. Update your description, gallery, hours, or products — changes reflect on your mini-website right away.
  3. Your category decides the layout (a restaurant's site leads with its menu, a hotel's leads with amenities, and so on), but you can still customize colors, photos, and content.

💳 Plans & Billing

How to upgrade a subscription
  1. Go to the Pricing page, or use the Upgrade Plan link on your dashboard or subscription tab.
  2. Choose monthly or yearly billing, and tell us whether you already have a website — it changes your price.
  3. Pick a plan and complete checkout via MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo Money, or card.

Standard is a one-time payment that keeps your listing live for exactly one month — it never auto-renews. When it ends, simply renew or upgrade whenever you're ready; nothing is deleted in the meantime, your listing is just hidden from search until you do.

How to connect an existing website
  1. When creating your listing (or on the pricing page before you subscribe), choose "I already have a website" and enter its URL.
  2. We link to it from your SpotGH listing and periodically check that it's still live.
  3. Businesses with their own website pay a lower plan price than businesses using a SpotGH-built mini-website, since we're not building and hosting a site for you.

Changed your mind later? You can update this from your business's Edit page at any time.

✨ AI Tools

How AI usage works

SpotGH uses AI to save you time on writing, not to replace your judgment — you always review and can edit anything it generates before it's published.

  • Generate Description — writes a business description from your name, category, and city. Edit it to sound like you before saving.
  • Generate Meta Title/Description — writes the text search engines show for your listing.

AI-generated text is a starting point — nothing is posted publicly until you approve it. (Review replies are written by you directly — there's no AI assist there yet.)
