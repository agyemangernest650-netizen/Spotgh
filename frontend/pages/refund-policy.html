Refund Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This is a starting template, not legal advice. Review it against Ghana's consumer-protection rules and Paystack's own merchant terms before going live.

1. No Auto-Renewal

SpotGH subscription plans (Starter, Pro, Enterprise) are billed for a single fixed period — one month or one year, depending on what you choose at checkout. We do not automatically charge your card again when that period ends. Instead, your business listing simply reverts to the Free tier until you choose to renew or upgrade. There's no risk of a surprise charge.

2. Free Trial

Eligible new business owners get a one-time, 30-day free trial of Starter-tier features with no payment required. Since no payment is taken for a trial, there's nothing to refund — if you decide not to continue, simply let the trial lapse and your listing reverts to the Free tier automatically.

3. When Refunds Are Available

Because our plans don't auto-renew and you choose exactly what you're paying for at checkout, refunds are generally limited to the following situations:

  • Duplicate or accidental charge — if you were charged twice for the same plan period, or charged in error.
  • Failure to deliver the paid service — if a technical fault on our end meant your paid-for plan features (e.g. higher upload limits, analytics) were never actually applied to your account.
  • Unauthorized transaction — if your card was charged without your authorization, subject to our fraud review.

4. When Refunds Aren't Available

  • Change of mind after a plan period has started;
  • Partial refunds for unused days within a paid period — since plans don't auto-renew, you're only ever billed for the period you selected;
  • Refunds requested more than 14 days after the charge.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email support@spotgh.com with your account email and the Paystack transaction reference (visible in your Payment History). We aim to respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method via Paystack and may take 5–10 business days to reflect, depending on your bank.

6. Cancelling a Listing

You can stop using a paid plan at any time — since there's no auto-renewal, there's nothing to "cancel" going forward. If you'd like your business listing removed from the Platform entirely, you can do this from your dashboard or by contacting us.

7. Contact

Billing questions? Reach us at support@spotgh.com.
