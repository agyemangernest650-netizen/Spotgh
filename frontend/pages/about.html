Every business deserves to be found

SpotGH exists so that a hairdresser in Kumasi, a chop bar in Tamale, and a boutique hotel in Accra can all be discovered online, trusted by customers, and given the tools to grow — without needing a developer or a big marketing budget.

Why we started SpotGH

Most Ghanaian businesses are easy to find in person but nearly invisible online. A customer moving to a new area, a tourist looking for somewhere to eat, or someone searching "salon near me" too often comes up empty — not because the business isn't good, but because it was never listed anywhere searchable. Meanwhile, getting a proper website built usually means hiring a developer, paying upfront, and waiting weeks.

SpotGH closes that gap. Any business can create a searchable, professional listing in minutes — complete with a free mini-website if they don't already have one — and start showing up where customers are already looking.

Our mission

To make it possible for every business in Ghana, regardless of size or budget, to be discoverable, credible, and easy to reach online. We measure success not by how many listings exist, but by how many real customer connections — calls, WhatsApp messages, bookings, orders — those listings generate.

Why businesses trust SpotGH

Verification, not just sign-up

Verified businesses go through an identity check, so the badge on a listing actually means something to customers.

Real reviews only

Reviews come from real users and can be flagged and moderated — we don't manufacture or sell reputation.

No lock-in

You own your content. If you already have a website, we link to it and charge you less for the privilege — we're not trying to replace it.

Transparent pricing

Plans are published, prices don't hide fees, and Standard is a one-time payment with no surprise auto-renewal.

How SpotGH helps you grow

Beyond a listing, SpotGH gives business owners the tools that usually cost extra elsewhere: a health score that tells you exactly what to improve, analytics that show what's working, AI-assisted descriptions and replies, a lead marketplace, deals and events promotion, and a referral program that rewards you for bringing in other businesses. All of it is built to save time, not add another dashboard to babysit.

Where we're headed

We're starting in Ghana because that's where we understand the market best — but the platform is built with expansion in mind. As SpotGH grows, the goal stays the same: make it as easy as possible for a real business to be found by a real customer.
